Government Healthcare Empanelments

A.P. Superspeciality Dental Hospital is committed to extending quality dental care through recognised government and institutional healthcare networks. Our association with CGHS, NEHS, and Police Arogya Bhadratha helps eligible beneficiaries access comprehensive dental and specialised treatment through established healthcare programmes.
